Dexterous Robotics Platform

Intelligent hands for research and industry

France's reference platform for dexterous robotic manipulation — open middleware, datasets, teleoperation tools, and applied research for labs and industry across Europe.

Four pillars. One platform.

Several capable dexterous hands are commercially available today. The bottleneck is the infrastructure around them — teleoperation tools, trained skills, curated datasets, and the expertise to connect them to a real use case. That is what we build.

01 // TELEOPERATION

VR control interface

An operator wears a VR headset, sees through the robot's first-person camera, and controls the hand using natural hand movements in real time. Every session is recorded as a timestamped manipulation trajectory, ready to use as training data.

Real-timeROS26DoFTraining-ready output
02 // DATA

Manipulation datasets

We collect and curate grasp and manipulation sequences on reference hardware — covering a wide range of objects, tasks, and difficulty levels. Fully documented, labeled by task type and object class, available under open access for research and commercial licence for industry.

03 // MIDDLEWARE

Open skills library

A hardware-agnostic ROS2 abstraction layer with a unified interface across supported hand models, and a growing library of packaged motion primitives. Each skill ships with documentation, a demo launch file, and simulation model support. Dual-licensed: open for research, commercial for industry.

Applied use-case projects

Focused contracts — three to six months — from problem definition through to a trained policy running on physical hardware. For labs that want to move fast and industrial teams that need a proof of concept before full deployment.

Built for researchers, engineers, and innovators.

TARGET: LABS

  • Access to hardware, open datasets, and production-ready middleware — so your team focuses on research, not infrastructure. We welcome collaboration proposals, early-access requests, and PhD-level partnerships.

TARGET: INDUSTRIAL

  • If your process involves assembly, handling, or any manipulation task currently done by hand — we run a focused pilot and deliver a working proof of concept, structured for innovation teams.

TARGET: ECOSYSTEM

  • Hardware vendors, system integrators, and research institutions building on a shared open platform. Open to co-development, distribution, and consortium arrangements.

About

Stonedrum Robotics is a Paris-based platform dedicated to dexterous robotic manipulation. We provide the infrastructure the field is missing — teleoperation tools, open middleware, curated datasets, and applied expertise — built openly, hardware-agnostic, and designed to scale with the community.
