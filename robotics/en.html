Dexterous Robotics Platform

Intelligent hands for research and industry

We are building France's reference platform for dexterous robotic manipulation — open middleware, training datasets, VR teleoperation tools, and applied research for labs and industrial teams across Europe.

Four pillars. One platform.

Several capable dexterous hands are commercially available today. The bottleneck is the infrastructure around them — teleoperation tools, trained skills, curated datasets, and the expertise to connect them to a real use case. That is what we build.

01 // TELEOPERATION

VR control interface

An operator wears a VR headset, sees through the robot's first-person camera, and controls the hand using natural hand movements in real time. Every session is automatically recorded as a clean, timestamped manipulation trajectory — ready to use as training data.

02 // DATA

Manipulation datasets

We collect and curate grasp and manipulation sequences on reference hardware — covering a wide range of objects, tasks, and difficulty levels. Published with full documentation, labeled by task type and object class, and available under open access for research and commercial licence for industry.

03 // MIDDLEWARE

Open skills library

A hardware-agnostic ROS2 abstraction layer with a unified interface across supported dexterous hand models, alongside a growing library of packaged motion primitives — pinch grasp, power grasp, lateral pinch, in-hand rotation, and more. Each skill ships with documentation, a demo launch file, and simulation model support. Dual-licensed: open for research, commercial for industry.

04 // RESEARCH

Applied use-case projects

Focused contracts — typically three to six months — from problem definition through to a trained policy running on physical hardware. For labs that want to move fast and industrial teams that need a proof of concept before committing to full deployment. Eligible projects can be co-funded through BPI France and European research programmes.

Built for researchers, engineers, and innovators.

TARGET: LABS

  • Access to hardware, open datasets, and production-ready middleware — so your team can focus on research questions rather than infrastructure. We welcome collaboration proposals, early-access requests, and PhD-level partnerships.

TARGET: INDUSTRIAL

  • If your process involves assembly, handling, insertion, or any manipulation task currently done by hand, we can run a focused pilot and deliver a working proof of concept. Structured to move at the pace of innovation teams.

TARGET: ECOSYSTEM

  • Hardware vendors, system integrators, and research institutions who want to build on a shared, open, maintained platform. We are open to co-development, distribution, and consortium arrangements.

About

Stonedrum Robotics is a Paris-based platform dedicated to dexterous robotic manipulation. We provide the infrastructure that the field is missing — teleoperation tools, open middleware, curated datasets, and applied expertise — built openly, hardware-agnostic, and designed to become more useful the more people build on it. We work with research institutions, engineering schools, and industrial partners across France and Europe.
