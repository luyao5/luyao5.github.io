ABOUT

Stonedrum Robotics, a dexterous manipulation platform

Based in the Paris Region, Stonedrum Robotics designs integrated solutions that connect dexterous hands, robotics software, data services, and embodied AI workflows.

Company profile

Stonedrum Robotics

The Robotics activity of Stonedrum SAS focuses on vendor-neutral architectures for dexterous manipulation: hardware selection, software abstraction, data capture, experimental validation, and team transfer.

Mission

Our role is to turn a variety of dexterous-hand and embodied-AI hardware infrastructures into usable systems: algorithms, software, data, documentation, and decision protocols.

Hardware infrastructure

Dexterous hands, arms, capture devices, sensors, and simulation from several public ecosystems.

Software

SDK, HAL, primitives, ROS 2 / Python interfaces, and validation tooling.

Data

Capture, cleaning, annotation, and use pipelines for manipulation data.

Transfer

Documentation, technical workshops, experimental scoping, and onboarding support.
