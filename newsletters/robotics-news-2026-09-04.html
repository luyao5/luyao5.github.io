3. Dynamique des mains dextres

X Square Robot lance TwinDEX : main de manipulation « jumelle » à 3 doigts et 9 degrés de liberté — une pour la collecte portable, une pour le robot, la manipulation « sans robot » devient viable
ManipulationITHome · 02-09
Le 2 septembre, l'entreprise d'IA incarnée X Square Robot a dévoilé TwinDEX, une paire de mains de manipulation dextres jumelles à trois doigts et neuf degrés de liberté : l'une est un terminal d'acquisition portable, l'autre est montée sur le robot réel. Les deux mains sont conçues de manière miroir pour rester identiques en cinématique (degrés de liberté, axes d'articulation, rapports de liaison), en mécanique de contact (matériaux, géométrie, capteurs tactiles), en apparence visuelle et en précision articulaire. Selon les évaluations internes, l'efficacité d'acquisition « sans robot » atteint 5,3× celle de la téléopération sur robot réel, et les données « sans robot » remplacent à près de 100 % les données de téléopération à l'entraînement. L'entreprise affirme que cela bouleverse la « pyramide de données » : l'entraînement à la manipulation dextre ne dépend plus de données de robot réel coûteuses — la collecte « sans robot » à grande échelle devient une voie praticable.
Wuji Hand 2 montée sur la plateforme TRON 2 de LimX Dynamics : validation complète d'une manipulation bimanuelle mobile dans une pharmacie de médecine chinoise
Main dextreLeaderobot · début septembre
La plateforme mobile à deux bras TRON 2 de LimX Dynamics, équipée de la main à cinq doigts Wuji Hand 2 de Wuji Technology, a réalisé une validation complète en pharmacie : préhension d'herbes, pesée en suspension, broyage et emballage. Wuji Hand 2 offre 20 degrés de liberté actifs en entraînement direct (≈ 180×80×40 mm, préhension statique 12 kg, opposition complète et inclinaison latérale) ; TRON 2 affiche ±0,5 mm de répétabilité. L'objectif affiché : livrer à l'utilisateur une combinaison bras-main « prête à l'emploi » — mécanique, alimentation, communication et données — face au constat que « les mains deviennent très performantes, mais rares sont ceux qui les font vraiment travailler ».
Les mains dextres prennent le devant de la scène : marché chinois estimé à 70 200 unités en 2026 (+265,6 %)
MarchéZhonggong Net · 01-09
À la cérémonie d'ouverture de la Conférence mondiale de la robotique 2026, onze mains dextres ont serré la main de onze invités : des X-Hand M1 de Yuequan, équipées de 472 cellules tactiles. Selon GGII : ventes chinoises 2025 ≈ 19 200 mains (+236,8 %), 2026E ≈ 70 200 (+265,6 %), > 430 000 à l'horizon 2030 (CAGR 2026-2030 ≈ 57,8 %). Financement : 16,88 Mds RMB en 2025 sur le secteur ; les sept premiers mois de 2026 dépassent déjà ce total (~28,5 Mds RMB fin juillet). Le secteur passe d'une « course aux paramètres » à la stabilité, l'utilité et la capacité de déploiement.

PrécommandeSecurities Daily · 03-09
Le 3 septembre, Galbot et l'université Tsinghua ont ouvert les précommandes du GALBOT ET1, présenté comme le premier robot « agent natif incarné » au monde : 1 230 mm, 30 kg seulement, enveloppe souple, moteurs Unitree. Côté logiciel, il embarque le modèle de « petit cerveau » généraliste AstraBrain-WBC entraîné sur 100 000 heures de mouvements humains : il apprend des gestes par observation directe et coopère en temps réel avec d'autres robots. NormesCNR (Chine) · 27-08
Publié par le département Science & Technologies de la MIIT le 24 août, le projet de Guide est soumis à consultation publique du 25 août au 23 septembre. Le système de normes couvre six volets : fondamentaux communs ; cerveau de type biologique & calcul ; membres et composants — incluant modules de perception (vision, force, toucher) et modules d'exécution (rotation, linéaire, main dextre) ; robot complet & systèmes ; applications ; sécurité & éthique. Un guide similaire pour les interfaces cerveau-machine (BCI) est également soumis à consultation.

La carte de consolidation (mise à jour du 1er septembre) identifie cinq tendances : ① intégration verticale (matériel + modèles IA + pipeline de données) ; ② expansion géographique — équipes européennes et israéliennes entrant aux États-Unis par acquisitions, consolidation chinoise attendue ensuite ; ③ croisement automobile — Mobileye, Tesla et Hyundai fondent l'intelligence humanoïde sur leurs piles de conduite autonome ; ④ « Physical AI » comme thèse d'investissement et d'acquisition ; ⑤ pression chinoise — > 2 Md$ levés par les humanoïdes chinois en 2025, ~90 % des livraisons mondiales. Financements de référence : NEURA (~1,4 Md$ en série C, ≈ 7 Md$), Figure (> 2 Md$ cumulés), Unitree (IPO 610 M$), Apptronik (série A cumulée 935 M$, > 5,5 Md$).

1. Headline events — capital-market milestones

XPENG's robot unit raises > $900M first round (> $6.3bn post-money): a record single private round in China's embodied-AI sector
FundingGuotai Junan (robot weekly) · Aug 30
On Aug 24, XPENG announced the first funding round of its humanoid business: over $900M at a post-money valuation above $6.3bn, led by IDG Capital with GSR Ventures and strategic investments from Tencent and Alibaba — a record for a single private round in China's embodied-AI sector, and the largest deal in this window. The IRON robot targets volume production by end-2026, with commercial launch in China and overseas in 2027.
Hesai's founders launch Sharpa: RMB 4.5bn+ raise (~$0.67bn), from dexterous hand to a "zero-modification" robot restaurant
FundingTencent News · Aug 29
On Aug 28, Sharpa — founded in late 2024 by Hesai's founding team (Li Yifan, Xiang Shaoqing, Sun Kai) — disclosed its first raise: over RMB 4.5bn at a post-money valuation of ~RMB 22bn, from Alibaba, Meituan, Tencent, JD.com, Transsion, Sequoia China, Qiming Venture Partners, Meituan-Longzhu and Guanghe. Products: SharpaWave tactile hand (22 active DoF, in production since Oct 2025), a 70-DoF full-body humanoid platform "North" and the CraftNet manipulation model; in June 2026 it co-released an NVIDIA Isaac GR00T humanoid reference design with Unitree. That same day, the world's first "zero-modification, fully autonomous, 24/7" robot restaurant opened with DQ in Shanghai (55-step Blizzard in ~6 min).
Dexterous-hand funding hits RMB 31.25bn in 2026 (1.85× the full-year 2025 total): capital concentrating on leaders — Linkerbot, AGILINK, Xynova
CapitalShell Finance / Sina · Aug 26
By Aug 25, China's dexterous-hand sector logged 78 deals worth ~RMB 31.25bn (1.85× the full-year 2025 total), increasingly concentrated: Linkerbot closed 7 rounds in one year (valuation up from RMB 1.5bn to 15bn); AGILINK — spun off from AGIBOT — 4 rounds in ~5 months (RMB 10bn); Xynova 4 rounds in under a year (RMB 8bn). Key-component players are also highly valued: PaXini at ~RMB 15bn after two rounds, Wuji Technology at ~RMB 5bn. Reports caution that "10,000-unit scale" claims mix cumulative deliveries, order books and capacity — industrialization is still unproven.

3. Dexterous hand dynamics

X Square Robot unveils TwinDEX "twin" dexterous hand (3 fingers, 9 DoF): one wearable for collection, one fitted on robots — "robot-free data" drives dexterous manipulation
ManipulationITHome · Sep 2
On Sep 2, embodied-AI firm X Square Robot unveiled TwinDEX, a pair of twin three-finger, nine-DoF dexterous manipulation hands: one is a wearable data-collection device, the other is mounted on real robots. The collection and execution sides are mirror-designed to stay identical in kinematics (DoF, joint axes, link ratios), contact mechanics (materials, geometry, tactile sensors), visual appearance and joint accuracy. Official evaluations show robot-free collection is 5.3× more efficient than real-robot teleoperation, and robot-free data replaces close to 100% of teleop data in training. The company says this reshapes the "data pyramid": dexterous-manipulation training no longer hinges on costly real-robot data, making large-scale robot-free collection a viable path.
Wuji Hand 2 on LimX Dynamics' TRON 2: full "arm-hand integrated mobile manipulation" validation in a Chinese-medicine pharmacy
Dexterous handLeaderobot · early Sep
LimX Dynamics' dual-arm mobile platform TRON 2, fitted with Wuji Technology's five-finger Wuji Hand 2, completed a full validation run in a pharmacy: herb picking, suspended weighing, grinding and packing. Wuji Hand 2 delivers 20 active fully-direct-drive DoF (~180×80×40 mm, near-human size, 12 kg static grip, full opposition and lateral swing); TRON 2 offers ±0.5 mm repeatability. The stated goal: hand users a "ready-to-use" arm-hand combo with mechanics, power, communication and data all integrated — answering the industry gap that "hands are getting very capable, but few teams actually put them to work."
Dexterous hands take center stage: China market seen at 70,200 units in 2026 (+265.6%)
Market dataZhonggong Net · Sep 1
At the 2026 World Robot Conference opening, eleven dexterous hands shook hands with eleven guests — Yuequan X-Hand M1 units carrying 472 tactile sensing cells each. GGII data: 2025 China sales ≈ 19,200 hands (+236.8%); 2026E ≈ 70,200 (+265.6%); over 430,000 by 2030 (CAGR 2026-2030 ≈ 57.8%). Funding: RMB 16.88bn for the sector in 2025; the first seven months of 2026 already surpassed that (~RMB 28.5bn by end-July). The industry is shifting from a "spec race" to stability, practicality and deployment capability.
Linkerbot spans standardization and premium: affordable O6 (360 g, 50 kg) vs full-direct-drive O30 (20 joints)
Product strategy163.com · Aug 31
Analysis of Linkerbot's two-track strategy shown at WRC: the O6 "affordable general hand" (6 active + 5 passive DoF, 360 g, max load 50 kg) for humanoids, logistics and industrial assembly; and the O30 direct-drive model with 20 fully independent active joints "from finger root to fingertip." Reported figures: ~RMB 260m revenue in 2025, orders above RMB 400m in early 2026, a 50k-100k unit delivery target this year, a ~RMB 1.5bn Series B in February plus a B+ in April, and reports of a >RMB 40bn valuation goal. Open question: as integrators (Unitree with its Dex series) build their own hands, how much of the market stays with third-party suppliers?

4. Humanoids & product launches

Galbot opens pre-orders for "Yinhe Xingzai" (GALBOT ET1): a native-agent small biped trained on 100,000 hours of human motion data
Pre-orderSecurities Daily · Sep 3
On Sep 3, Galbot and Tsinghua University opened pre-orders for the GALBOT ET1, billed as the world's first "native embodied agent" robot: 1,230 mm tall, only 30 kg, soft-shell body, Unitree motor solution. It runs Galbot's own AstraBrain-WBC general-purpose "little brain" foundation model trained on 100,000 hours of human motion — it learns gestures by watching people and improvises with other robots in real time. Unlike "bolted-on" agents, neural inference and motor closed-loop control are co-designed in one real-time frame, making thought and action near-synchronous. Targets: home, retail, guidance.
Galbot Store lands in Hong Kong: first three fully autonomous robot retail stores open, first stop outside mainland China
Go-globalChina News Service · Sep 2
On Sep 1, Galbot opened its first three fully autonomous "Galaxy Capsule" (Galbot Store) outlets in Hong Kong — Hung Hom, Wan Chai waterfront and Kai Tak Sports Park — its first overseas location. Humanoid store manager "Xiaogai" handles restocking, picking, checkout and multilingual voice interaction (Mandarin, Cantonese, English) with no remote human control. Financial Secretary Paul Chan made the first purchase and noted Galbot Store already runs ~200 stores across nearly 50 mainland cities. "Xiaogai" won three golds (household, catering, supermarket events) at the 2nd World Humanoid Robot Games.
AGIBOT and Chimelong sign for the world's first large embodied-AI theme park: robot circus, robot animal parade, robot-staffed hotel at Hengqin
DeploymentXinhua Finance · Aug 24
On Aug 23, AGIBOT and Chimelong Group signed a strategic agreement at Chimelong Spaceship Park, Hengqin (Zhuhai): jointly building a large-scale embodied-intelligence theme park — immersive robot theme park, robot animal parade, large robot circus show, cyber-themed parade and a robot-staffed hotel — plus a joint lab for embodied AI in tourism. Counterpoint Research data shows global humanoid shipments exceeded 22,000 units in H1 2026 (+~300% YoY); AGIBOT shipped ~9,700 units, over 43% of the global share. Chimelong welcomes 43M+ visitors a year.
Unitree registers Chengdu subsidiary "Chengdu Yuchen": three bases (western pilot, film-robot, education) and two centers (scenarios, data collection)
ExpansionJinguan News · Sep 3
On Sep 2, Unitree's wholly-owned subsidiary Chengdu Yuchen Technology was registered in Chengdu. The "three bases, two centers" plan comprises a western robot pilot base (humanoids, quadrupeds, wheeled-arm robots), a film-robot base, an education-robot base, plus a robot scenario-innovation center and a robot data-collection center in Jinjiang district — and a plan to build the world's largest product-launch center on Chunxi Road. The five leading Chinese embodied-AI firms (Unitree, UBTECH, AGIBOT, Galbot, RobotEra) are now all present in Chengdu, whose core AI industry surpassed RMB 150bn in 2025.

5. Data collection & data services

Mifei ships its 20,000th MEgo device: over 1M hours of "robot-free" data, Tencent Robotics X deal and third-party data sales
Data servicesXinhua Finance · Sep 1
On Aug 31, Mifei — AGIBOT's data arm — announced the 20,000th MEgo no-robot data-collection device and over one million hours of high-quality no-robot (non-teleoperation) data: ~500,000h Ego bare-hand, ~350,000h Ego+wrist, ~150,000h UMI gripper, spanning 22 scene categories, 10,000+ real environments and 500+ tasks — now offered as data products/services, which it calls an industry first at the million-hour scale. A no-robot data partnership with Tencent Robotics X Lab was announced on site. It also introduced HandPose high-precision hand reconstruction (PA-MPJPE error −62% vs next best, jitter −93%, internal) and its ManiEval quality-grading system.
Tachin scales the ecosystem around TachinGlove: open tactile dataset and developer program with Orbbec, Noitom, ZHAOWEI and more
Tactile dataSecurities Daily · Aug 31
Tachin — which unveiled at WRC what it calls the world's first flexible e-skin tactile data-collection glove (TachinGlove: ~1,000 sensing points per hand, mN sensitivity, 3D force, 75 g, wireless with 6h battery) — has open-sourced an initial full-hand tactile dataset (grasping, folding, assembly, insertion) with SDK on Hugging Face, and launched a "bionic e-skin & embodied-tactile ecosystem developer program" bringing together Orbbec (vision), Noitom (motion capture), a teleoperation player and ZHAOWEI (precision drives) — an "Android play" to set the tactile data standard ahead of the VLA → VTLA (vision-touch-language-action) transition. Pro (IMU/EMF fusion, multi-cam Ego) and Lite (finger-stall form) versions ship in October. Global open tactile datasets remain under 1/10,000th of visual data volume.
August's "tactile wars": PXCap Pro (PaXini), Jingzhigan isomorphic glove, TachinGlove, LivUMI Dex (Lingsheng) — collection gloves go upmarket
TactileTencent News · Sep 3
A roundup of August tactile-glove launches: PaXini's PXCap Pro (Aug 14; 325 g, 17 DoF, 165° wrist camera, 17 encoders at ±0.15°); Jingzhigan's "true" isomorphic tactile glove (Aug 21; 1,500+ points across the hand, normal and shear force, AI super-resolution to ~10,000 points, 1-gram threshold); TachinGlove (Aug 20); Lingsheng's LivUMI Dex exoskeleton multimodal glove (direct joint encoders + near-hand vision + IMU + DataCube hardware sync); Yaole positions its G1 as a "standardized industrial consumable" with automated knit production. Acquisition devices are becoming the infrastructure gateway for embodied data.
PaXini: over 1M tactile chips shipped, ~RMB 4bn raised in total, HQ moved to Beijing — touch moves to the center of the control loop
Tactile chipsScience & Technology Daily · Sep 3
PaXini reports more than one million self-developed tactile sensing chips shipped in the past year and a fully vertical stack: materials, structures, dedicated chips, calibration and algorithms, extending to hands, robots and embodied data/models. After a RMB 1bn strategic round (cumulative ≈ RMB 4bn, valuation > RMB 10bn), it relocated its HQ to Beijing and completed a shareholding reform. At WRC it unveiled the GEN4 FUSE 6D tactile chip (mN sensitivity, synapse-like encoding into "tactile tokens"), PX6AX GEN4 sensors and the first multi-dimensional plantar sensor PX-FOOTRIX. BYD and JD.com are business and equity partners.
Lingyu closes a multi-hundred-million RMB pre-Series A: Tsinghua-linked team building "robots + data + cloud", deployed at JD's 7FRESH
Data infrastructureSTAR Market Daily · Aug 26
On Aug 26, Lingyu Intelligent — an embodied-AI data-infrastructure provider — announced a multi-hundred-million RMB pre-Series A (its third round this year) led by regional funds. Proceeds fund robot production expansion, upgrades to its real-robot data pipeline and its cloud operation platform. Founded in early 2025 by a largely Tsinghua team, Lingyu runs around one hundred robots in continuous operation at 7FRESH (JD supermarkets): every grasp, handover and shelf contact logs force data back to a growing physical-interaction database — a "robot + real data + cloud platform" loop.
Figure launches the Index dataset: over 16 million real-world videos collected
DatasetGuotai Junan (robot weekly) · Aug 30
On Aug 25, Figure AI CEO Brett Adcock announced the Index dataset, which already aggregates over 16 million real-world videos spanning household, logistics, restaurant and industrial tasks — feeding end-to-end embodied models. Combined with its Figure 03 robots' continuous warehouse operations, Figure is betting on both "volume production" and "real-data scale."

6. Key component suppliers

Humanoids enter the "volume & delivery" phase: actuators and reducers convert into revenue first
Supply chainChinese financial media (synthesis) · Sep 3
Industry syntheses note actuators (motor + reducer + screw) account for over 50% of a humanoid's hardware cost and the dexterous hand 20-25%. Harmonic reducers: Leaderdrive holds >60% of the humanoid segment; Zhongding has 150k units/year capacity and an order book of ~300k, with OEM nominations (Zhongqing, Fourier). RV reducers: Shuanghuan (supplier to major integrators), Julun with a BYD order of ~100k units/year. Planetary roller screws: XCC (Wuzhou Xinchun) has entered Tesla's Optimus chain; Bote is investing RMB 1.85bn (2.6M units/year planned). Frameless/hollow-cup motors: Inovance supplies Tesla, Unitree and Xiaomi; Leisai shipped 120k+ frameless motors in 2025, covering ~80% of mainstream humanoid makers. Key-component localization: >75% by end-2025 (CAICT).
Dexterous-hand drives & actuators: MOONS' (hollow-cup), ZHAOWEI (motor+module), Tuopu, Sanhua, Lens Technology ramping
ComponentsChinese financial media (synthesis) · Sep 3
Chain observations (press/brokerage syntheses, indicative only): MOONS' is a reference hollow-cup motor supplier for dexterous hands; ZHAOWEI leads "motor+module" integration for dexterous-hand motors; Tuopu is in small-batch delivery for linear/rotary actuators and hand motors (in-house planetary roller screws and frameless motors); Sanhua is ramping biomimetic motor-actuator lines; Lens Technology, a tier-1 supplier to Tesla Optimus, provides head modules, joint modules, dexterous hands and full assembly; Changying delivered 860k precision humanoid parts in H1; Hengli targets 500k screws/year by 2027 (~100k today); Best Precision signed a strategic partnership with Unitree (300k units/year targeted in 2027-2028). Dexterous-hand and linear-actuator components are now in small-batch supply to leading Chinese integrators.
Force sensors & joint modules ahead of scale-up: Keli 1,000+ orders/month, Guangyang's joint module first sample in August
ComponentsEast Money (synthesis) · Sep 3

The chain is moving from technical validation to small-batch delivery.

7. Industry trends & global news

MIIT opens consultation on the National Humanoid Robot Industry Standards Guide (2026 ed.): at least 100 key standards by 2028
StandardsCNR (China) · Aug 27
Issued by the MIIT Science & Technology Department on Aug 24, the draft guide is open for public comment from Aug 25 to Sep 23. The standards system spans six blocks: common foundations; brain-like & computing; body parts & components — including perception modules (vision, force, tactile) and execution modules (rotary, linear, dexterous hand); complete robots & systems; applications; safety & ethics. Target: at least 100 key standards by 2028 and 200+ enterprises adopting them. A parallel guide for brain-computer interfaces is also under consultation.
NDRC: coordinated rollout of embodied-AI training grounds and high-quality real-robot data acquisition systems
PolicyNDRC (official site) · Aug 28
At its Aug 28 monthly press conference, NDRC spokesperson Li Chao said the state will anchor on embodied-AI training grounds and national AI application pilot bases so robots iterate in real scenes and form application loops around real demand: building high-quality real-robot data acquisition systems (to ease the "data hunger"), supporting vision-language-action and world-model routes, and building technical standards that lower cross-embodiment adaptation costs. He also cautioned against blindly following trends.
Tesla Optimus Gen 3: Fremont pilot ramp, ~1,000 units deployed internally, Sept 3 Cybercab event as a signal window
GlobalTesorb (public-information synthesis) · as of Sep 1
Third-party tracking (public information as of late August): Optimus Gen 3 is ramping at Tesla's Fremont pilot factory with ~1,000 robots deployed, mostly for internal learning rather than productive work; estimated manufacturing cost $50k-100k per unit, enterprise pricing above $100k, with Musk's consumer target of $20k-30k no earlier than 2028-2029. The analysis stresses the absence of a paying external customer so far — while Figure already bills ~$25/robot-hour to BMW and others. Tesla's Sep 3 Cybercab event is seen as a window for new Optimus V3 signals.
2026 global humanoid ranking: Figure first, Tesla second, Unitree third — Unitree gives back gains after its listing
GlobalTeahose (data-driven ranking) · as of Aug 24
A third-party ranking (capital, signals, real deployments) places Figure AI first — Series C over $1bn (~$39bn valuation), in-house Helix model, an 11-month BMW Spartanburg pilot handling 90,000+ parts and expanding to Leipzig — ahead of Tesla (scale) and Unitree, a rare profitable player: ~RMB 1.7bn revenue in 2025 (+335%) and RMB 1.152bn in H1 2026 (+48.5%); listed on the STAR Market on Aug 19, its market cap retreated from an extreme peak to ~$34bn by Aug 24. Next come Physical Intelligence (the model layer, ~$5.6bn), Apptronik and Boston Dynamics. The report flags the value battle between the model layer and the hardware layer.
