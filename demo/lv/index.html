AI+AR Intelligent Museum Guide System

Combining AI agents with AR glasses to provide immersive museum experiences and enhance art and cultural dissemination.

Introduction
  • • The intelligent museum guide utilizes advanced optical waveguide AR glasses or AI smart glasses to automatically recognize exhibits via an AI agent, providing descriptions through voice or text. The AI agent connects to a customized large language model for real-time natural communication, giving visitors the feel of a personal human guide.
  • • This system effectively improves traditional museum displays by offering faster, more convenient information access (no need to search for plaques), accurate multi-language commentary, and natural Q&A to satisfy visitor curiosity without needing human guides.
  • • It attracts younger audiences with digital technology while making exhibitions accessible to visually or hearing-impaired visitors, enhancing the social impact of cultural popularization.
