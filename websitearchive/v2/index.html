Our Core Technologies

Cutting-edge innovation at the intersection of AI, Augmented Reality, and human sensory experience.

Proprietary Agentic AI

Unlike generic models, our AI agents are trained on private cultural archives and proprietary datasets. They provide accurate, context-aware, and spontaneous conversational guidance, acting as expert curators for every visitor.

Immersive Visual Augmented Reality

We deploy advanced Augmented Reality visualization on lightweight glasses. Featuring 6DoF spatial tracking and edge-streaming, our software overlays seamless, high-fidelity digital content onto the physical world without disrupting the viewing experience.

Haptic Interaction

Beyond sight and sound, we engage the sense of touch. Our innovative piezoelectric sensor technology enables "Haptic Augmented Reality," allowing users to feel textures, movements, and semantic cues, creating a truly multi-sensory connection.

Solutions for Culture & Tourism

Transforming how people connect with art, history, and live performance.

Enriched Museum Experience

Forget crowded labels and rigid audio guides. Our AI-powered Augmented Reality guide recognizes exhibits instantly, offering multilingual storytelling and answering customized questions in real-time. Each visitor receives a personalized journey through history and art.

Immersive Stage Companion

For opera, theater, and concerts. We provide discrete, in-view Augmented Reality subtitles and contextual information, breaking down language barriers and enhancing comprehension without distracting from the stage. Experience every nuance, in any language.

Digital Inclusion & Accessibility

Technology for humanity. We utilize visual transcripts, descriptive audio, and haptic feedback to make cultural institutions accessible to visitors with visual or hearing impairments. Art belongs to everyone.
